ABOUT NOVACART

A polished front-end ecommerce experience built for real-world practice.

NovaCart demonstrates product APIs, persistent carts, pagination, account flows, and responsive design in a clean static project.

Fast Catalog

Products are loaded from JSON and rendered with efficient search, filter, and pagination controls.

Persistent Cart

Cart items and quantities are saved in browser storage, so the shopping flow survives refreshes.

Account Flow

Registration and login include validation, duplicate checks, and secure session handling.

Built with practical standards

The app keeps concerns separated across markup, styling, catalog logic, and authentication. It is easy to connect to a real backend later.
